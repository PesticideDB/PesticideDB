About PesticideDB

PesticideDB is a curated database and annotation platform for pesticide-degrading microorganisms, genes, proteins, and pathways. It centralizes pesticide degradation data to support sustainable agriculture, environmental health, and discovery of microbial genes, enzymes, proteins, and degradation routes.

PesticideDB organizes pesticide information into two complementary categories: pesticides with experimentally reported microbial degradation evidence and pesticides for which no validated microbial degradation evidence was identified during curation.
