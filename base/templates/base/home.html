About PesticideDB

The Pesticide Biodegradation Database (PesticideDB) is a curated online platform related to the microbial biodegradation of pesticides. It is an initiative to centralize pesticide degradation data, supporting sustainable agriculture and environmental health. Check the paper at: doi.org/10.1186/s40793-024-00575-4.

Notice: Our database compiles all peer-reviewed literature we identified that reports microbial degradation of pesticides. Numerous candidate pesticide-degrading organisms and enzymes have been described, and we present this information to encourage further exploration of the global diversity of such microbes and enzymes. Although the database includes many enzymes linked to pesticide breakdown, most remain poorly characterized, and their expression does not necessarily equate to actual degradation activity. We therefore emphasize the need for additional studies to conclusively verify the microbial degradation of these pesticides.
