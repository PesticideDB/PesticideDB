Pesticide Classification

Our database contains information on {{ pesticide_count }} pesticides.

{{ pesticide_count }} pesticides are sourced from the FAO/WHO Codex Alimentarius Pesticide Database. These pesticides are listed by the Codex Alimentarius Commission because they are subject to Codex Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs). Codex MRLs are international standards established to ensure food safety, protect consumer health, and support fair trade.

Pesticides included in the Codex list are those most widely used in agriculture and therefore require consistent monitoring of their residues in food and feed.

An additional 2 pesticides have been included because they are widely studied in the scientific community. These compounds are not covered in the Codex database, but their classification is crucial for biodegradation research. By classifying them, we make their nature easier to understand, enabling researchers to apply microorganisms, enzymes, or proteins for their degradation.

Why Classification Matters

Classifying pesticides helps researchers:

  • Understand chemical and structural properties of pesticide compounds
  • Identify potential microbial or enzymatic degradation pathways
  • Compare biodegradation research across different pesticide classes

We do not claim that this is the only or the best classification method. Instead, it represents a practical and appropriate approach designed to make pesticide biodegradation research more accessible and applicable.
