How to Interpret Genome Annotation Results

Recommended Interpretation

The table shows the closest PesticideDB protein hit from DIAMOND and the broader enzyme-family support from HMMER. Treat each row as a candidate annotation, not proof of pesticide degradation.

A result is strongest when percent identity is high, alignment length is substantial, DIAMOND E-value is low, and the DIAMOND enzyme class agrees with the HMMER family.

General Warnings

Low identity hits, short alignments, pseudo genes, or disagreement between DIAMOND and HMMER should be manually checked before functional naming.

The downloaded CSV includes extra interpretation columns such as original query product, pseudo flag, interpretation confidence, warnings, and final interpretation for detailed review.
