About PesticideDB

Our database currently has {{ pesticide_count }} pesticides information and {{ microorganisms_count }} species of microorganisms that have been curated from scientific literatures. Our mission is to advance knowledge and accessibility of microbial pesticide degradation by integrating data on microorganisms, enzymes, genes, degradation pathways, and analytical evidence under one unified framework.

The database is updated every six months to keep up with new publications and add more pesticides.

If you have any information about microorganisms and proteins that we could use to improve the database, please submit a request to add, remove, or modify. You can also leave any comments in this form and we will get back to you.
